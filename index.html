About Us

A School Built on Purpose & Dedication

Founded under the Jagath Janani Krishi & Gramina Abhivrudhi Samsthe, our school is committed to delivering quality Kannada-medium education to children in rural Kalaburagi. We prepare students for Navodaya and Sainik schools through structured coaching and a supportive residential environment.

  • Experienced and dedicated teaching staff
  • Residential facility with meals included
  • Specialized Navodaya & Sainik School exam coaching
  • Strong track record of student selections
  • Safe, disciplined, and nurturing campus

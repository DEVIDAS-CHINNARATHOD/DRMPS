How to Apply

Simple Admission Process

Getting your child admitted is straightforward. Follow these four steps to secure a seat.

1

Call or Enquire

Call us or fill in the online enquiry form with your child's details and class preference.

2

Visit the School

Schedule a visit to the campus to meet the teachers and understand the facilities in person.

3

Submit Documents

Provide birth certificate, previous school records, and a passport-size photo of the student.

4

Confirm Admission

Complete the fee payment and confirm your child's seat. Hostel booking is done at this stage.

Limited Seats Available

Don't miss the opportunity. Summer camp begins April 1, 2026. Contact us now to secure your child's seat.
